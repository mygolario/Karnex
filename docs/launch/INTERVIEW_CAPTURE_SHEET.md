## 📝 During the Call — Quick Capture Tips

- **Record** (phone voice memo) — ask permission first
- **Write 1–2 words per column** during the call, expand after
- **Don't defend / explain** — just "ممنون، خیلی ارزشمنده" and probe: "بیشتر باز کن..."
- **Silence is okay** — let them think after Q3 and Q5
